Golden Temple

It is a temple dedicated to Lord Ganesha, the remover of obstacles. It is located in Amritsar, Punjab, India.

Mysore Palace

The palace is located in Mysore, Karnataka, India. It is one of the most famous palaces in India and is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Varanasi Temple

The temple is located in Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh, India. It is one of the oldest temples in India and is dedicated to Lord Shiva.

Jaipur Palace

The palace is located in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India. It is one of the most famous palaces in India and is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Charminar

The Charminar is a historical monument located in Hyderabad, Telangana, India. It was built in the 16th century and is a symbol of the city.

India Gate

India Gate is a famous war memorial located in New Delhi, India. It was built in memory of Indian soldiers who sacrificed their lives.

Qutub Minar

Qutub Minar is a historical monument located in Delhi, India. It is one of the tallest brick minarets in the world.

Red Fort

The Red Fort is a historical fort located in Delhi, India. It was built by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in the 17th century.

Gateway of India

The Gateway of India is a famous monument located in Mumbai, Maharashtra. It was built to commemorate the visit of King George V and Queen Mary.

Hawa Mahal

Hawa Mahal is a beautiful palace located in Jaipur, Rajasthan. It is also known as the Palace of Winds.

Ajanta Caves

Ajanta Caves are ancient Buddhist rock-cut caves located in Maharashtra, India. They are famous for their beautiful paintings and sculptures.

Konark Sun Temple

The Konark Sun Temple is a famous historical temple located in Odisha, India. It is dedicated to the Sun God.

Hampi

Hampi is a historical village located in Karnataka, India. It is famous for its ancient temples, monuments and ruins.

Meenakshi Amman Temple

Meenakshi Amman Temple is a famous Hindu temple located in Madurai, Tamil Nadu. It is known for its colourful towers and architecture.

Victoria Memorial

Victoria Memorial is a beautiful marble monument located in Kolkata, West Bengal. It is a famous historical and cultural landmark.
